# Real user questions: RTI Mate

Written the way my user types them. User: Resident welfare association secretary in Tambaram who wants road-repair spending details from the municipality.

These 10 questions become my first evaluation set in Week 3. Question 6 is not answerable from my documents: the product must say "I don't know".

| # | Question | Type | Where the answer is (document, page or section) | More than one page? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What is the time limit for a public authority to reply to an RTI application? | simple | RTI Act 2005, Section 7(1) | No |
| 2 | What is the RTI application fee in Tamil Nadu, and how can it be paid? | simple | Tamil Nadu RTI Rules (fee and payment provisions; confirm the current rule text) | No |
| 3 | What information is exempt under Section 8(1)(j)? | simple | RTI Act 2005, Section 8(1)(j) | No |
| 4 | Our RWA in Tambaram wants details of road-repair contracts and bills for our ward for the last 3 years. Who should we ask, how should we word the questions, and what if they don't reply? | multi-step (several pages) | RTI Act 2005, Sections 6, 7 and 19; Tamil Nadu municipal records | Yes |
| 5 | Compare the first appeal and the second appeal under the RTI Act. | comparison | RTI Act 2005, Sections 19(1) and 19(3) | Yes |
| 6 | Has the municipality received my RTI application? | unanswerable: must reply "I don't know" | none: not in any document | - |
